Dana Okafor: Rarely. Maybe a co-marketing partner we're building a shared dashboard with, but that's an edge case. Default to viewer, make editor a deliberate, annoying-on-purpose choice. If it's too easy someone will fat-finger it.

Dana Okafor: That matters more than I expected. When I send a link to a client, my CSM wants to know if the champion actually looked at it before the renewal call. Even a simple "opened 3 times, last on Tuesday" would change how we prep. I don't need full forensics, just signal.

Dana Okafor: Right. Compliance is Wei and the security folks' problem. For me it's "did the buyer engage."
